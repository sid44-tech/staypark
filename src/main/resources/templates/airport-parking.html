How does this process work?

Simply put – It is QUICK and EASY!

Step 1:
Make your reservation to park your car at the hotel/parking facility of your choice and pay online with our secure site.

Step 2:
Print your confirmation letter, which you will receive via email, and bring it with you on your drop off date.

Step 3:
On the drop off date park your car at the hotel/parking facility and show your confirmation letter to the attendant/front desk. Make sure you obtain the hotel's telephone number at this time (Will come in handy for Step 4). Catch the shuttle to the terminal.

Step 4:
Upon return, call the hotel/parking facility from the Airport Terminal and ask them to send the shuttle to pick you up.

Step 5:
When you arrive at the hotel/parking facility get in your car and head home.
