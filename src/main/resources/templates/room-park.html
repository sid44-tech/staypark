HOW IT WORKS
Hotel Room + Parking

  • This option allows the customer to book a hotel room along with parking for their vehicle. For example, if your trip is 7 days long and you need to park your car for this duration while you are away, BUT you need a hotel room the day before you depart, you would select the Hotel Room + Parking: Start of Trip option.
  • Select your departure location (by clicking the red arrow) and enter the hotel check in/out dates as well as your car drop off/pick up date and time (if applicable). Note: If choosing a hotel room for the "start & end of your trip", there will be no need to enter a car pick up time. If choosing a hotel room for the "start of your trip" then the car drop off date is automatically populated for you with the date you check out from the hotel room. You will see a complete breakdown of your booking on the search results page as well as the checkout page.
Hotel Room Only

  • This option allows the customer to just book a Hotel Room ONLY. There is no additional parking included in this option.
